Privacy Policy — Sonori

Last updated: 2026-09-11

Sonori ("the App") is a real-time speech interpretation app. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: we run no servers and collect nothing about you beyond anonymous crash reports and usage statistics — plus whatever you choose to e-mail us. Translation is performed by Google's Gemini API using an API key that you provide.

Who we are

The App is published by 1XP Inc. ("we"). We do not operate any backend servers that receive your content, and we have no account system.

What stays on your device

The following are stored only on your device (in the iOS Keychain or local app storage) and are never transmitted to us:

  • Your Google Gemini API key (encrypted in the Keychain). Where it goes, how to verify that, and how to keep it safe: API key safety.
  • Browser history, bookmarks, and last-opened page.
  • App settings (target language, voice, translation context, output route).

If you pair an Apple Watch, your API key and settings are synced directly to your Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity (device-to-device). They do not pass through us.

What is sent to Google (Gemini API)

To translate, the App sends audio you capture (microphone, or media playing in the in-app browser/player) and the resulting text to the Google Gemini API, authenticated with your own API key. This is a direct connection between your device and Google under your own Google account/key.

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Terms:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

The App does not record, store, or retain your audio. It is streamed for translation and discarded. We never receive it.

The optional "AI Brief" feature sends the topic keywords you type to the same Gemini API (with Google Search grounding) using your key.

Other network connections

  • Web browsing: in browser mode, the App loads the web pages and media you navigate to, exactly like a browser. Those sites are subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Google AI Studio: tapping "Get a free key" opens Google AI Studio in Safari so you can create a key. That is a normal Safari session with Google.
  • Feature configuration: at launch the App fetches a small static configuration file (feature flags) over HTTPS. This is a plain download — no personal data, identifiers, or content are sent in the request.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Firebase)

To find out why the App crashes or misbehaves, and which features are actually used, the App uses Google's Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting) and Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics). This is everything that is sent to Google's servers:

  • On a crash: the stack trace, App version, device model and iOS version, and the interpreting settings in effect (capture mode, target language).
  • Usage statistics: when an interpreting session starts, ends or fails and how long it ran, the capture mode (microphone / browser / Apple Watch), the target language, and whether the feedback or review screen was opened. Google Analytics adds its automatic basics to these: standard events such as app open, app update, screen changes and time in app; device model, iOS version, App version and language setting; and a country-level approximate location inferred from the connecting IP address (the IP address itself is not stored). Everything carries a random app-instance ID generated per installation (it disappears when you uninstall).

Never sent: audio, transcripts or translations, translation context, your Gemini API key, personal information such as name or e-mail, the advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location (there is no location permission). Nothing is used for advertising and nothing is linked across apps or websites. Retention follows Google's Firebase policy (crash reports 90 days, usage statistics 2 months — the minimum we can set).

Feedback e-mail

"Send feedback" in Settings opens your own Mail app with the App version, device model and iOS version pre-filled in the body. Nothing is sent until you press send; when you do, that message (including your sender address and whatever you wrote) arrives at developer@1xp.com. We use it only to handle your request, do not keep it once the request is closed, and never pass it on. Write to the same address if you want it deleted.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your audio, transcripts or settings to ourselves.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, and no analytics beyond the crash reporting and usage statistics described above.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We have no user accounts.

Microphone

The App uses the microphone only while you are actively translating, to capture speech for real-time translation. Audio is not saved.

Children

The App is not directed to children. Because it includes an unrestricted web browser, it is rated 17+.

Your choices

  • Remove your API key anytime in Settings ("Delete key").
  • Clear browser history/bookmarks in the browser UI.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.

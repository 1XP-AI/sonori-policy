Privacy Policy — Sonori

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Sonori ("the App") is a real-time speech interpretation app. This

policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: **we run no

servers and collect nothing about you.** Translation is performed by Google's

Gemini API using an API key that you provide.

Who we are

The App is published by 1XP Inc. ("we"). We do not operate any backend

servers that receive your content, and we have no account system.

What stays on your device

The following are stored only on your device (in the iOS Keychain or local

app storage) and are never transmitted to us:

  • Your Google Gemini API key (encrypted in the Keychain).
  • Browser history, bookmarks, and last-opened page.
  • App settings (target language, voice, translation context, output route).

If you pair an Apple Watch, your API key and settings are synced **directly to

your Watch** over Apple's WatchConnectivity (device-to-device). They do not pass

through us.

What is sent to Google (Gemini API)

To translate, the App sends audio you capture (microphone, or media playing

in the in-app browser/player) and the resulting text to the **Google Gemini

API, authenticated with your own API key**. This is a direct connection

between your device and Google under your own Google account/key.

This data is processed by Google under **Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini

API Terms**:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

The App does not record, store, or retain your audio. It is streamed for

translation and discarded. We never receive it.

The optional "AI Brief" feature sends the topic keywords you type to the same

Gemini API (with Google Search grounding) using your key.

Other network connections

  • Web browsing: in browser mode, the App loads the web pages and media you

navigate to, exactly like a browser. Those sites are subject to their own

privacy policies.

  • Google AI Studio: tapping "Get a free key" opens Google AI Studio in

Safari so you can create a key. That is a normal Safari session with Google.

  • Feature configuration: at launch the App fetches a small static

configuration file (feature flags) over HTTPS. This is a plain download — **no

personal data, identifiers, or content are sent** in the request.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your data to ourselves.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We have no user accounts.

Microphone

The App uses the microphone only while you are actively translating, to

capture speech for real-time translation. Audio is not saved.

Children

The App is not directed to children. Because it includes an unrestricted web

browser, it is rated 17+.

Your choices

  • Remove your API key anytime in Settings ("Delete key").
  • Clear browser history/bookmarks in the browser UI.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
